Exercise & Eat Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't have servers, and the app doesn't send your data anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Apple Health is read only, only with your permission, and only for workouts.
  • Meal photos are checked on your device and never saved or uploaded.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Who we are

Exercise & Eat is an iPhone app for logging food and exercise, made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

Information stored on your device

To work out your daily targets and show your progress, the app stores the following on your iPhone:

  • Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal.
  • What you log: foods, portions and times, exercise sessions, and the vitamin and mineral amounts calculated from them.
  • Your settings, such as custom nutrient targets and appearance.
  • For meals you've linked to a photo: a short list of what Apple's on-device image recognition saw in the photo (for example “food, pizza, plate”), so the app can recognise the same meal next time. The photo itself is not kept.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or your computer) if you have backups turned on, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads your workouts from Apple Health so exercise recorded on your Apple Watch or in other apps appears in your log. The app does not write anything to Health. Health data is used only inside the app to show your activity. It is never sent off your device, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or marketing. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Camera and meal photos

If you choose to log a meal from a photo, the app asks for access to your camera, or you can pick a photo from your library using Apple's photo picker. The photo is analysed on your iPhone with Apple's built-in image recognition. It is never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else, and it is not saved: only the short list of what it shows is kept, as described above. You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings › Exercise & Eat.

Purchases and subscriptions

Exercise & Eat PRO is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store. Payment, billing and your Apple Account details are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never see your payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device to unlock PRO features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because everything stays on the device, we have no information to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can edit or delete anything you've entered in the app, or delete the app to remove it all.

Nutrition information

Vitamin, mineral and other nutrient values are estimates based on public data from the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and daily targets follow the U.S. Dietary Reference Intakes. They are for general information and are not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

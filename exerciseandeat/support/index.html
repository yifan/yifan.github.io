Exercise & Eat Support

Help with logging, PRO and your data

Need help?

  • Email eae@yifanlabs.dev. We usually reply within two working days.
  • Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Logging food and exercise

  • Log something: tap +, then tap a food or exercise. It's added to today straight away.
  • Choose a portion or duration: touch and hold a food or exercise, then pick the amount.
  • Find things faster: use the search bar or the category chips. The grid button switches between bigger and smaller tiles.
  • Remove an entry: on Today, touch and hold the entry in Today's log, then choose Delete.

Daily targets

Calorie and macro targets are worked out from the profile and goal you choose (lose, maintain or gain). Change them any time in the You tab. Targets are general estimates, not medical advice.

Apple Health

In the You tab, tap Apple Health to import workouts recorded by your Apple Watch or other apps. If nothing appears, check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Exercise & Eat and allow access to workouts.

Exercise & Eat PRO

  • PRO adds the smart meal and workout planner, macro suggestions and detailed vitamin and mineral tracking.
  • PRO is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends.
  • Cancel or change plan: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Exercise & Eat. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app? Open the PRO screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you enter is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email eae@yifanlabs.dev or use Send feedback in the You tab.
